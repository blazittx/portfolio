## Astronaut Character

The astronaut character was implemented as a fully physics-driven system where interaction quality was prioritized over animation-based solutions. Each hand is controlled independently, making movement and interaction deliberate and physically grounded in a zero-gravity environment.

The system supports independent thumbstick control for each hand, physical grabbing of objects, and momentum-based movement. Players can pull, push, and fling themselves through the station by interacting with the environment rather than relying on traditional locomotion.

The player controller handles input routing, movement constraints, and force application, while a separate hand controller manages grabbing, releasing, collision handling, and interaction forces. This separation allowed faster iteration and clearer responsibility between systems.

## General Environment

The station environment is built as a true zero-gravity space where all objects behave consistently under physics simulation. The goal was to avoid scripted motion and instead rely entirely on physics to make the station feel active and believable.

#### Requirements

1. Create a completely zero-gravity environment.
2. Spawn props dynamically inside the station and apply subtle rotational forces so they naturally hover and drift.


#### Zero-Gravity Setup

Gravity is disabled globally so all rigidbodies rely on applied forces and momentum rather than constant downward acceleration.

Props are defined as a prefab list and spawned at runtime to keep the station from feeling static. Instead of hardcoding spawn coordinates, the level defines **spawn volumes** using box colliders placed throughout the station.

At runtime, the spawner collects these box colliders, picks one at random, generates a random point inside its bounds, and spawns a random prop prefab at that position. After spawning, each prop gets a small randomized rotational force (and optionally light impulse force) so the movement stays subtle but constantly “alive” in zero gravity.

### Wire Connect Puzzle

This task is a physics-based cable connection puzzle built on top of the hand-grab system. The interaction is fully physical and avoids UI-driven connections to keep the experience grounded in the game world.

:::columns
:::column width=35
![Wire connect puzzle room](https://blazitt.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fprod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fb1ce6ad4-df17-4518-9922-7b0ca1a8de55%2F827ba77e-de56-47d1-90b2-d11d249a906d%2FFrame_5510.png?table=block&id=f11f34b8-d981-424f-bebf-671818c36e8f&spaceId=b1ce6ad4-df17-4518-9922-7b0ca1a8de55&width=640&userId=&cache=v2)

*Wire connection puzzle room*
:::

:::column width=60

1. The player must be able to grab and carry objects.
2. Male and female wire parts snap together when in proximity.
3. A visible cable is rendered between the fixed start point and the moving plug.
4. Plugs and sockets are color-matched to enforce correct connections.
5. Incorrect connections trigger an electrical failure that kills the crew.

:::
